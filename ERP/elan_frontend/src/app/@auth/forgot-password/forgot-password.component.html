Reset Your Password

To begin the password reset process, please enter your registered User ID. After clicking the “GET OTP” button, our system will send a secure 6-digit OTP to your registered email address.

This OTP helps us verify your identity and ensure that only authorized users can update the password. After receiving the OTP, you will be able to set a new password of your choice.

Make sure to keep your email inbox accessible, and enter the OTP within the allowed time limit to avoid expiration. If the OTP expires, you can request a new one anytime.
